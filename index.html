ABOUT THIS GAME

404: SPACE ESCAPE — you drifted off the map. Survive the void, blast enemies, and rack up points while you find your way back.

Controls

WASD / Arrow Keys or Mouse/Touch to move. SPACE, Click, or Tap to shoot.

Pickups

Green Crystal — instant bonus points.

Orange Diamond — Rapid Fire: boosts fire rate for 5 seconds. Grab more before it expires to stack it even faster.

Gold Orb — Shield: absorbs the next 3 asteroid hits (glowing ring shows remaining charge).

Pink 1UP — instantly grants an extra life. Rare — grab it fast!

Waves & Perks

Clear enough enemies to advance the wave. After each wave, pick a permanent upgrade: Rapid Fire, Multi-Shot, Piercing Rounds, Thruster Boost, Salvage Magnet, Reinforced Hull, Combo Mastery, or Score Surge.

Boss Asteroid

Every 5th wave spawns a massive glowing boss rock. Destroy it and it splits into 3 smaller asteroids.

Near Miss

Skim past an asteroid without getting hit for a small score bonus.

Combo

Kill enemies quickly in succession to build a score multiplier, up to your Combo Mastery cap.

Easter Egg

Fly into a corner of the arena for a surprise.
