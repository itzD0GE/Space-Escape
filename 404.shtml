HOW TO PLAY

404: SPACE ESCAPE — you drifted off the map. Clear each wave, beat its boss, pick an upgrade, and climb the board.

Controls

Keyboard: WASD / Arrows move, Space fires, Shift dashes, E drops the Nova Bomb, P pauses, M mutes.

Mouse: the ship follows the pointer, which is locked to the game while you play (Esc pauses and frees it). Hold left button to fire, right-click to dash, middle-click for the bomb.

Touch: drag to fly and fire. Use the DASH and BOMB buttons.

Gamepad: left stick moves, A / RT fires, B / bumpers dash, X / Y bomb, Start pauses.

Dash, Graze, Bomb

Dashing makes you briefly untouchable. Skimming past enemies and bullets is a graze: it scores and charges the Nova Bomb, which clears every bullet and damages everything on screen.

Donuts and Sweets

Matcha: points. Cinnamon: Rapid Fire stack. Vanilla: 3 shield charges. Strawberry: 1UP. Blueberry: temporary Multi-Shot.

Mochi doubles fire rate, Dango doubles points, Sushi widens pickup range, Taiyaki makes you immune. All are temporary.

Damage

A shield absorbs a hit. Without one you lose a life and your most recent weapon upgrade, then get a moment of immunity.

Perks and Evolutions

Choose one perk after every boss. Certain pairs evolve: Chain Lightning + Piercing becomes Storm Lance, Homing + Spread becomes Swarm Missiles, Rapid Fire + Thrusters becomes Coolant Loop, Combo + Score Surge becomes Jackpot Protocol.

Waves

Every wave ends with a boss. Bosses enrage at half health and telegraph their heavy volley. From wave 4, elite enemies and wave mutators appear.

Modes

Daily Challenge: everyone flies the same waves once a day. Rival Contract: a three-wave score race on identical waves; kills send attack squads to your rival.

Easter Egg

Fly into a corner of the arena for a surprise.
